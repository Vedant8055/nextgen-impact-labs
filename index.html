THE IDEA BEHIND THE INITIATIVE

Young minds.
A wider horizon.

We believe young people should not simply inherit the future. They should have meaningful opportunities to help shape it.

NEXTGEN IMPACT LABS is a youth-led social innovation forum founded by Kiyansh Kapse and Yuvaan Kedare. Rooted in Nashik, the initiative aims to bring people, ideas and practical action together around challenges that matter to communities.

From learning and digital access to environmental responsibility, health awareness and youth innovation, our focus is on starting thoughtful conversations, exploring useful solutions and learning from every step.
