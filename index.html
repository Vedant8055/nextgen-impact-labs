01 / THE WHY

Where young ideas
find their purpose.

〰

We believe age should never limit the size of an idea—or the good it can inspire.

NextGen Impact Labs brings young thinkers, families, educators and communities closer to the questions that matter. It is a space to explore challenges, shape practical concepts, learn by doing and share solutions with a wider audience.

Beginning in Nashik and looking towards North Maharashtra, our purpose is to encourage a culture of youth leadership, responsible innovation and meaningful community participation.

Curiosity first Purpose always Progress together

05 / THE INITIATIVES

YOUTH PROJECT 01
RIVER HEALTH · ECOLOGY

Goda Yuva

Keeping Our Rivers Young

Goda Yuva is a youth-led concept that draws attention to healthier river spaces. Through a hands-on demonstration, it explores improved water flow, groundwater recharge, biodiversity and the role of thoughtful riverbed design.

WATER STEWARDSHIP · INNOVATION

JalKiyan

Clean Purposeful Water

JalKiyan presents a simple, visual exploration of water retention and healthier riverbeds. Its demonstration models invite conversation about cleaner water, sustainable water systems and practical ways to think about our shared resources.
